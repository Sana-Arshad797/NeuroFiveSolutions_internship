essential to their safe operation. AI systems can be attacked in several
ways, including data poisoning, where attackers corrupt training data,

## Agent 2 - Editor (Final Draft)

Artificial intelligence systems increasingly power critical areas such
as healthcare, finance, and infrastructure, making strong cybersecurity
essential to their safe operation. AI-specific threats include data
poisoning, where attackers corrupt training data to bias a model's
behavior, and adversarial examples, where deliberately crafted inputs
cause a model to misclassify or fail in unexpected ways. Because AI
systems frequently handle sensitive personal or business data, securing
both the data pipeline and the trained model itself is critical, not
optional. As AI adoption grows across industries, organizations that
build security into their AI development process from the start — rather
than treating it as an afterthought — will be far better positioned to
avoid costly breaches and maintain user trust.
